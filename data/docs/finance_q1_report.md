# Nimbus Retail Inc. — Q1 Finance Report

## Overview
Nimbus Retail Inc. closed Q1 with total global revenue of $42.3M, up 8% quarter-over-quarter.
Growth was driven primarily by North America and India, while Europe showed early signs of
softening demand toward the end of the quarter.

## Regional Breakdown
- North America: $18.1M (up 11% QoQ)
- Europe: $11.4M (up 3% QoQ)
- India: $7.2M (up 19% QoQ)
- Rest of World: $5.6M (up 4% QoQ)

## Notes on Europe
Europe's growth slowed in March due to a temporary pause in marketing spend while the regional
team migrated to a new ad platform. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) in Europe rose 14% during
this transition. Leadership expects this to normalize in Q2 once the new platform is fully
integrated.

## Product Mix
The Home & Kitchen category remained the largest contributor across all regions, followed by
Electronics. Europe saw a notable dip in Electronics sales, partly attributed to a delayed
product launch that was pushed from March to April.

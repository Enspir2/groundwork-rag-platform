# Nimbus Retail Inc. — HR Policy: Leave and Time Off

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly
at a rate of 1.5 days/month. Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried over to the following year.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive
days requires a medical certificate submitted to HR within 5 working days of return.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid, in line with statutory requirements. Paternity leave is 2 weeks
paid, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Compensation Confidentiality
Employee compensation details, including salary bands, bonus structures, and performance ratings,
are classified as confidential HR information and are not to be shared outside of HR and direct
reporting managers.

# Nimbus Retail Inc. — Product Note: Electronics Line Recall (May)

## Summary
The Aura Smart Plug (SKU: NR-AS-220), originally scheduled to launch in Q1 but delayed to
April due to supplier certification issues, was recalled voluntarily on May 6th after a
small number of units in the European batch (manufactured at the Lisbon facility) were found
to have a wiring defect causing overheating under sustained load.

## Scope
The recall affected approximately 14,000 units, all shipped to the European market. No units
sold in North America, India, or Rest of World were affected, as those regions were served by
a different manufacturing batch.

## Financial Impact
The recall required a full refund/replacement program estimated at $1.8M, along with a pause
in Electronics category marketing in Europe during May while replacement stock was prepared.

## Resolution
A corrected batch passed re-certification in late May and began shipping to European customers
in June. Early sell-through data suggests demand for the product remains strong once available.

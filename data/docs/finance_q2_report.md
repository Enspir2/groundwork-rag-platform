# Nimbus Retail Inc. — Q2 Finance Report

## Overview
Total global revenue for Q2 was $40.8M, down 3.5% quarter-over-quarter. The decline was
concentrated almost entirely in Europe, while North America and India continued to grow.

## Regional Breakdown
- North America: $19.0M (up 5% QoQ)
- Europe: $8.9M (down 22% QoQ)
- India: $7.9M (up 10% QoQ)
- Rest of World: $5.0M (down 11% QoQ)

## Europe Deep Dive
Europe's revenue decline in Q2 was driven by three compounding factors:

1. The delayed Electronics product launch (originally due in Q1) shipped in April but faced
   a manufacturing defect in the first batch, resulting in a voluntary recall in early May.
2. The new ad platform migration mentioned in the Q1 report was completed, but the new platform's
   targeting underperformed the old one for the first six weeks, keeping CAC elevated.
3. A regional competitor launched an aggressive discounting campaign in Germany and France
   starting in April, which Nimbus did not match until late May.

## Rest of World Note
The Rest of World decline was primarily currency-driven and is not considered a demand issue.

## Outlook
Leadership expects partial recovery in Europe in Q3 as the recalled product is reissued and
the ad platform targeting improves, but does not expect a full return to Q1 levels until Q4.
